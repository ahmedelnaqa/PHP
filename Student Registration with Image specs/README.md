## This system facilitates student registration by ensuring uploaded images meet specific size requirements. A dedicated interface allows students to submit their photos, and the system automatically formats them to match university card dimensions, streamlining the printing process.

#### - Purpose: To streamline student registration with compliant photo submission.
#### - Functionality: Provides a student-facing page for image upload.
#### - Automation: The system programmatically resizes and formats uploaded images to the precise specifications of the university ID card.
#### - Benefit: Ensures uniformity and significantly speeds up the card printing process.
